# EvoPlatform Vision

## Purpose

EvoPlatform is an independent, general-purpose platform for building, evaluating, distributing, and evolving AI capabilities.

A capability may be a prompt, skill, agent, workflow, tool integration, evaluator, or a composed system.

## Problem

AI assets are often shared as isolated prompts or undocumented agent configurations. This makes them difficult to evaluate, secure, version, adapt across models, and operate reliably.

EvoPlatform treats AI capabilities as versioned engineering artifacts with explicit contracts, evidence, policies, and evaluation results.

## Principles

- Capability-first design rather than prompt-only collections.
- Model-neutral interfaces with provider-specific adapters.
- Evaluation before promotion.
- Security, privacy, and provenance by default.
- Composable prompts, skills, agents, workflows, tools, and evaluators.
- Local-first and self-hosted operation.
- Human approval for high-risk actions.
- Reproducible experiments and transparent limitations.
- Standards compatibility without copying external implementations.

## Scope

The platform is intentionally general. Initial domain packs may cover research, coding, business, data, security, Arabic language tasks, creative work, education, and infrastructure operations.

## Non-goals

- Replacing foundation-model providers.
- Claiming that one prompt is universally optimal.
- Automatically executing high-risk changes in production.
- Republishing proprietary or confidential system prompts.
- Building a closed ecosystem that prevents portability.

## Success criteria

The project succeeds when users can discover or create a capability, understand its requirements and risks, evaluate it on meaningful datasets, run it with a selected model, and promote or roll it back using evidence.
